The challenge

A client hired me to build a restaurant and small-chops delivery website. After launch, she told me about a problem that had been eating into the business for a long time, and said she'd be happy if anyone could take it off her plate.

She described it, and that problem became PrepMaster: turning each day's orders into kitchen prep sheets automatically.

The approach

I built it for her as a separate application. Then I thought: if one restaurant needs this, many do. So I turned it into my own product, a WordPress plugin with a Node.js and PostgreSQL backend, sold with Free, Pro and Business plans through Freemius.

PrepMaster

Kitchen prep automation for WooCommerce restaurants

PrepMaster is a product I designed and built from scratch for restaurants that take orders through WooCommerce. It turns the day's orders into ready-to-use kitchen prep sheets automatically, so the kitchen knows exactly what to prepare without tallying tickets by hand.

A WordPress plugin written in PHP hooks into WooCommerce order data and works with a Node.js backend and PostgreSQL database. Restaurants, packages and product mapping are managed from a dedicated admin area with its own dashboard, prep-sheet views, a health check and built-in debug tools.

Licensing, subscriptions and pricing run on the Freemius SDK, from license-key activation to plan upgrades: a Free plan to get started (1 restaurant, 20 orders a month, basic prep sheets), Pro at $89.99/year for growing restaurants with unlimited orders and auto product mapping, and Business at $249.99/year for up to 10 restaurants with analytics, report exports, email notifications and multi-user access.
